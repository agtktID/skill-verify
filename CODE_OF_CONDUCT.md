# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim to keep `skill-verify` precise, constructive, inclusive, and focused on reliable engineering. Participants should be respectful, assume good faith, explain disagreements clearly, and welcome corrections supported by evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets, and disruptive behavior that prevents technical collaboration.

## Enforcement

Report serious incidents privately to the repository maintainers. Public issues must not contain credentials, personal data, or exploit details that could put users at risk.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
